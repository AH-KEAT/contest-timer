配色标准 · Colour standard
Apple HIG 只用语义色表达状态：靛蓝 Indigo 等待/聚焦（3·2·1 准备）、绿 Green 进行顺利（演讲中）、 蓝 Blue 中性延续（加时/问答）、橙 Orange 注意收尾、红 Red 停止超时。切换浅色/深色会自动换成 Apple 的浅色版色值。
Apple maps colour to meaning, not decoration: indigo = waiting/focus, green = on track, blue = neutral continuation, orange = wrap up, red = stop. Light and dark each use their own Apple values.
